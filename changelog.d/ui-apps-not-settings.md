### Added

- The Apps tab of the Marketplace has Add custom app and Manage custom apps. A custom app is an MCP
  server that you add by its address or its command. On the Custom apps page you turn each one on or
  off, test it, sign in to it, edit it and remove it.

### Changed

- Connectors, such as GitHub, Slack, Discord, Telegram, 1Password and Bitwarden, and MCP servers are
  all apps now. You find them in Marketplace → Apps, under Your apps and More apps, and each one
  opens its page there.
- Server settings show only server settings. The MCP and Connectors sections are gone from them;
  everything that they did is in Marketplace → Apps.
- In the simple layout, the plug list shows Marketplace, then Apps, then your AI providers. Apps
  opens the Apps tab of the Marketplace, not the server settings.
- The Sign in button for an MCP server on an agent's card opens the Custom apps page.
- In the simple layout, Settings and Server settings open as their own view, with their own section
  list and a Back button. The Apps list and the bottom bar no longer show beside them.
- In the simple layout, an AI provider in the plug list opens its page in the Apps area, with its
  accounts, version and on switch.
