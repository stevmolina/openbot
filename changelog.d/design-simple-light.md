### Added

- Simple layout, on by default in this fork: a warm light theme and a calm layout. A bar under the
  sidebar replaces the left icon rail: your photo opens Settings, Usage, Help and Switch host; the
  chat, clock and plug buttons show your agents, your routines, tasks and schedule, and the
  Marketplace, connectors and AI providers. The agent panel has Details, Library and Computer tabs,
  with every other setting under More settings. Turn it off in Settings → General → Simple layout
  to get the standard layout back.
- `bun run package:light` builds OpenBot Light, a separate macOS app with its own data folder and no
  automatic updates.
