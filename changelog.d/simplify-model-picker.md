### Changed

- The model picker is simpler. Each model is one line with its name; point at it to read its
  description. The chosen model holds its controls on its row: the lightning turns fast mode on or
  off, and the blue chip opens the effort levels. Providers that are not connected follow the ready
  ones, dimmed with a dot. A long model list, such as an OpenCode catalog, keeps a filter field.
  The arrow keys move through the models, Right opens the effort levels, and Escape closes.

### Removed

- The search field above every model list and the Effort and Speed rows under the list. The effort
  chip and the lightning on the chosen model replace them.
